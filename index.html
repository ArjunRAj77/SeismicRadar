About SeismicRadar

SeismicRadar is a real-time global dashboard monitoring the earth's tectonic activity, with an interactive map of recent seismic events.

Data source

All data is pulled directly from the United States Geological Survey (USGS) live GeoJSON feed, refreshed automatically every 60 seconds.

How it works

  • Click any event to fly to its epicenter and open full details.
  • Filter by time range, minimum magnitude, or location; sort by recency, magnitude, or distance.
  • Circle size and color encode magnitude; depth is shown in the detail panel.
